# Prototype design decisions

## Confirmed
- Godot 2D, one player, WASD/arrow movement, small territory, one farm plot, resource gathering, basic nest and upgrades, territory scent marking.
- Start uncategorised following the virus, without Alpha/Beta/Omega traits.
- Scent ability is locked until a configured nest completion percentage, then visible scent markers/trails appear.
- Larger sphere of influence eventually reveals more other-character scent and increases scent tracking distance.
- Nest completion is displayed as 0–100%; a well-built nest increases individual happiness.
- Pack happiness requires a significantly higher threshold and is planned for multiplayer, not Stage 1.
- Happiness enables greater influence over other characters (later social interaction system).
- Pastel/soft romance-themed art and UI; simple placeholder graphics preferred.
- Population: Alpha 15%, Beta 75%, Omega 10%; only Betas can potentially shift classification to Alpha or Omega.

## Open decisions — use clearly labelled prototype defaults
- Nest scent unlock %; resource cost per nest improvement; how much happiness each upgrade gives.
- How influence is earned or calculated in Stage 1 when no other characters exist.
- Whether scent markings fade (optional idea).
- Virus transformation triggers; classification-change permanence.

## Not in first playable milestone
- Multiplayer, packs, NPC romance, rut/heat simulation, full virus evolution, regional expansion, competitive territory disputes.
